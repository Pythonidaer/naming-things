# Common antonyms

Reference for [Use consistent antonyms](https://pythonidaer.github.io/naming-things/chapters/8-consistency/#section-8-2-5). Source table: [Common antonyms](https://pythonidaer.github.io/naming-things/chapters/18-common-antonyms/).

Pick one opposite for a concept and keep it. When a row lists more than one antonym, choose the one that matches the operation. Do not invent a third. Prefer the usual pair, and prefer opposites that share a root when the meanings are equal (`connect` / `disconnect`).

Choices that need a decision, not a blend:

- `add` is `remove` or `subtract`.
- `push` is `pop` or `pull`.
- `enter` is `leave` or `exit`.
- `expand` is `collapse` or `contract`.
- `source` is `destination` or `target`.
- `set` is `get` when the pair means read and write, `unset` when it means remove a value, and `clear` when it means empty the value.

`get` / `set` appears in both directions because the same words name both sides of that pair.
